E-Mail Manager

Utility that scans inbox(es) and replies to messages with text, images, and attachments based on user-specified rules and schedule. Answer the questions below, then save — you can change anything later.

1. Which inbox should I work on?

Enter the Gmail or Google Workspace address, then connect it. Google will ask you to approve access.

2. Which e-mails should get a reply?

Rules are checked from top to bottom; the first rule that matches decides what happens. Keywords match whole words, are not case-sensitive, and can be separated by commas or new lines. Advanced: write /pattern/ for a regular expression.
